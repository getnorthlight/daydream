# Citing DayDream moments

The person should be able to find each thing you mention again, in DayDream or in their own apps. Cite in plain words, never with ids or links.

## Format

`(<when>, <app or place>: <document, conversation or site>)`

- **when**: use the reply's local time, such as "Today, 9:12 AM", "Yesterday afternoon" or "Tue 3:12 PM". In a recap, a block anchor like "Morning" is enough.
- **app or place**: as the reply gives it (Google Docs, Claude, Messages, Terminal). Never a bundle id.
- **what**: the document title, the person, the project or the site, shortened if long. Leave it out if the reply has none.

Examples:

- "You last had the pricing page open at 5:48 PM (Today, 5:48 PM, Google Docs: Tallybird Pricing Page)."
- "You asked Claude whether the trial should be 14 or 30 days (Today, 10:06 AM, Claude)."
- "You wrote to Maya that you'd be 10 minutes late (Today, 12:31 PM, Messages: Maya Chen)."

## Rules

- Cite what was recorded (titles, times, typed words), not what a note claims. If you are relying on a note, say "DayDream's note says...".
- Never say a message was sent: DayDream can't tell whether it went out. Say what was typed and where.
- When you quote the person's typed words, quote only the part the question needs, inside quotation marks, and cite where it was typed.
- Keep the ids for your own follow-up calls (`details` with `id`). Don't show them.
- If two items could be the one the person means, list both with citations and ask which one.
