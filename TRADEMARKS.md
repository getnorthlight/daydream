## What you may do

- Say truthfully that your project is based on, forked from or compatible with
  DayDream, for example "a fork of DayDream".
- Refer to DayDream by name in articles, reviews, tutorials and discussions.
- Build and run unmodified DayDream from this repository for your own use.

## What you may not do without written permission

- Distribute a modified version, or a build you made yourself, under the
  DayDream name or with the DayDream logo or icon.
- Use the name, logo or icon in a way that suggests your project, product or
  service is made, endorsed or supported by the DayDream authors.
- Use "DayDream", or a confusingly similar name, for another activity-history
  or AI-memory app, or in any way likely to cause confusion with this project.

## If you fork DayDream

If you distribute a fork, rebrand it before you ship it:

1. Pick a different app name and replace the user-facing name everywhere it
   appears (for example `CFBundleName` and `CFBundleDisplayName` in
   `packaging/Info.plist`, window titles and menu text).
2. Replace the icon and logo files with your own artwork.
3. Change the bundle identifier and the update feed settings, so your builds
   never update, or get updated by, official DayDream builds.
4. Keep the LICENSE, NOTICE and THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md files, as the licenses
   require.
