## Updating

DayDream looks for updates once a day, unless you turned that off, and downloads this one quietly. It installs the next time DayDream quits, or now if you choose Restart to Update in the menu bar menu. To look now, choose Check for Updates. Recording stops for the update and starts again when DayDream reopens.
